# Exercises: Semihosting

Do not look at the `solutions/` folder until you have attempted these exercises.

## Exercise 1: The Production Crash
A team of engineers finishes their project and prepares to ship it to a customer. They have been using Semihosting to print debug messages to their Eclipse IDE. They compile the code, flash it to the board using the ST-Link, unplug the ST-Link, plug in a 9V battery, and mail it to the customer. The customer complains that the device is completely dead and unresponsive.
**Task:** Explain exactly why the device is "dead".

## Exercise 2: When to use it?
Given the severe drawbacks of Semihosting, what is a valid scenario where a professional engineer might actually choose to use it over UART?
